### What the table shows

- **Category order (week 1).** The dentist (10/12) and the dinner (12/12) fall in the same window. Social beats errands, so it's *Recevoir à dîner*. The dentist gets its turn with the follow-up in week 10.
- **Category keyword (week 2).** "Office holiday party" matches *party*, a category keyword. So it takes the first social theme off cooldown, *Un café entre amis*. ("Holiday" is no longer a travel keyword. It turned holiday parties into airport weeks.)
- **Cooldown and related theme (week 3).** Dinner at Léa's: *Recevoir à dîner* was used 2 weeks earlier, so the engine takes its first related theme, *En cuisine*.
- **A theme keyword beats a category one (week 4).** "New Year's Eve at the Martins" is the year review, not a generic social week.
- **Default with cooldown (week 5).** ISO week 53's default, *Le bilan de l'année*, was just used, so its related *Les bonnes résolutions* comes forward a week.
- **Skips.** The weekly standup (recurring), the declined board review and Mom's all-day birthday reminder never steer a week.
- **Cooldown expiry (week 11).** *Recevoir à dîner* is allowed again after 10 weeks.
- **Seasonal themes keep their season.** 2026 is an even year, but the December seasonal defaults are not swapped.

**"Today" column.** With only 7 complete themes, the engine never picks an unwritten one. When all 7 are on cooldown, it relaxes the cooldown and takes the one used longest ago. The simulation caught a bug here: an unwritten theme slipped through. It's now fixed, with a test. A repeated theme still shows unseen entries, because each theme has 12 entries and a week uses 6.
